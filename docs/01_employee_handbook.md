# Najmah Retail Group — Employee Handbook (2026)

> Najmah Retail Group ("Najmah", "the Company") is a fictional UAE-based retail group used here as sample data. Any resemblance to real companies is coincidental.

## 1. About This Handbook
This handbook summarizes the main policies for employees of Najmah Retail Group across our Dubai head office and store network. Where this handbook conflicts with your signed employment contract or UAE law, the contract and the law take precedence. For benefits specifics, see the **Employee Benefits Guide**. For leave mechanics, see the **Leave & Attendance Policy**.

## 2. Working Hours
The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM with a one-hour break. Store-based staff follow shift rosters published two weeks in advance. During Ramadan, working hours are reduced by two hours per day for all employees, in line with UAE labour regulations.

## 3. Probation
New employees serve a probation period of six months. During probation, either party may terminate employment with 14 days' written notice. Annual leave accrues during probation but may only be taken after confirmation, except in exceptional cases approved by a line manager.

## 4. Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues and customers with respect, and protect Company and customer information. Acceptance of gifts from suppliers above AED 500 in value must be declared to the Compliance team. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed in writing.

## 5. Remote and Hybrid Work
Head-office roles are eligible for up to two remote days per week, subject to manager approval. Store and warehouse roles are on-site by nature. Remote work requires a stable connection and adherence to the **Data Protection Policy** when handling customer data off-site.

## 6. Grievances
Any employee may raise a grievance with their line manager or, where that is not appropriate, directly with HR. Grievances are acknowledged within 3 working days and resolved within 15 working days where possible. Retaliation against anyone raising a good-faith grievance is itself a disciplinary offence.

## 7. Exit
On resignation, confirmed employees give 30 days' notice unless the contract states otherwise. Final settlement, including any end-of-service gratuity calculated per UAE law, is paid within 14 days of the last working day. See the **Employee Benefits Guide** for gratuity calculation details.
