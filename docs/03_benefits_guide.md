# Najmah Retail Group — Employee Benefits Guide (2026)

Covers benefits beyond leave. Read alongside the **Employee Handbook** (Section 7 on exit) and the **Leave & Attendance Policy**.

## 1. End-of-Service Gratuity
Gratuity is calculated on the last basic salary, per UAE law, for employees who complete at least one year of continuous service:
- For the first five years of service: 21 days of basic pay for each year of service.
- For each year beyond five years: 30 days of basic pay for each year.

**Worked example:** An employee with a basic salary of AED 12,000 and 7 years of service receives (5 × 21 days) + (2 × 30 days) = 105 + 60 = 165 days. Daily basic pay is 12,000 ÷ 30 = AED 400, so gratuity = 165 × 400 = **AED 66,000**. Total gratuity is capped at two years' total pay.

## 2. Health Insurance
All employees and their first dependent receive Company-funded health insurance under the Dubai Health Insurance scheme. Additional dependents may be added at the employee's cost through the annual enrolment window each January.

## 3. Annual Air Ticket
Expatriate employees receive one economy-class return ticket to their home country each year after completing 12 months of service. Unused ticket allowance does not carry over and is not paid in cash.

## 4. Staff Discount
Employees receive a 25% discount on Najmah own-brand products and 10% on third-party brands, capped at AED 10,000 of discounted value per year. The discount is for personal use only; resale is a Code of Conduct violation (see Handbook Section 4).

## 5. Education and Training
The Company sponsors role-relevant certifications up to AED 8,000 per year, subject to manager approval and a 12-month retention commitment after completion.

## 6. Pension and Savings
Emirati employees are enrolled in the GPSSA pension scheme as required by law. A voluntary savings scheme is available to all employees, with the Company matching contributions up to 3% of basic salary.
