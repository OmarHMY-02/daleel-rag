# Najmah Retail Group — Data Protection Policy (2026)

This policy explains how Najmah handles personal data of customers and employees, aligned with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and systems that process personal data on behalf of Najmah. It is referenced by the **Employee Handbook** Section 5 (remote work) and Section 4 (code of conduct).

## 2. What We Collect
For customers: name, contact details, purchase history, loyalty activity, and, for online orders, delivery addresses and payment tokens (never full card numbers, which are held by our payment processor). For employees: contract, payroll, and performance data.

## 3. Lawful Basis and Consent
We process personal data on the basis of contract performance, legal obligation, or consent. Marketing communications are sent only to customers who have opted in, and every message includes an unsubscribe option. Consent can be withdrawn at any time through the account portal.

## 4. Data Subject Rights
Customers and employees may request access to their data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion where there is no overriding legal reason to retain it. Requests are answered within 30 days. Identity is verified before any data is released.

## 5. Retention
Customer transaction records are retained for 5 years to meet tax and audit obligations. Loyalty data is retained while the account is active and for 12 months after closure. Employee records are retained for 5 years after the end of employment.

## 6. Security
Personal data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is granted on a least-privilege basis. Staff handling personal data off-site must follow the remote-work safeguards in Handbook Section 5. Suspected data breaches must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours of discovery.

## 7. Third Parties
We share data with processors (payment, delivery, cloud hosting) under contracts that require equivalent protection. We do not sell personal data.
