# Najmah Retail Group — Customer Returns & Refunds Policy (2026)

This is a customer-facing policy for purchases made in Najmah stores and on the Najmah online store.

## 1. Return Window
Most items may be returned within 30 days of purchase with proof of purchase. Sale and clearance items may be returned within 14 days. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and in resalable condition.

## 2. Non-Returnable Items
For hygiene and safety reasons, the following are non-returnable unless faulty: underwear, swimwear, pierced jewellery, opened cosmetics, perishable goods, and personalised or made-to-order items. Gift cards are non-refundable.

## 3. Refund Method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds appear within 7 to 14 business days depending on the bank. Cash purchases above AED 1,000 are refunded by bank transfer within 5 business days for security reasons. Online purchases are refunded once the returned item is received and inspected at the warehouse.

## 4. Faulty Goods
If an item is faulty, customers are entitled to a repair, replacement, or refund regardless of the standard return window, in line with UAE consumer protection law. Faulty electronics are assessed within 7 business days.

## 5. Exchanges
Exchanges follow the same condition rules as returns. If the replacement item costs more, the customer pays the difference; if less, the difference is refunded to the original payment method.

## 6. Online Orders
Online returns can be booked through the Najmah account portal, which generates a free return label for UAE addresses. Items must be handed to the courier within 5 days of the label being issued. International orders are not eligible for free returns.

## 7. Loyalty Points on Returns
Loyalty points earned on a purchase are reversed when that purchase is refunded. See the **Loyalty Programme Terms** for how points accrue and expire.
