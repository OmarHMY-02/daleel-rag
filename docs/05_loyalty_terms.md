# Najmah Retail Group — Loyalty Programme Terms (2026)

The Najmah Rewards programme is free to join for any customer with a Najmah account.

## 1. Earning Points
Members earn 1 point for every AED 1 spent on eligible purchases. Own-brand Najmah products earn double points (2 points per AED 1). Points are credited within 48 hours of purchase. Purchases of gift cards do not earn points.

## 2. Membership Tiers
There are three tiers, reviewed every 12 months based on spend in the preceding year:
- **Silver:** the entry tier for all members. Standard earning.
- **Gold:** reached at AED 10,000 annual spend. Earns a 10% point bonus and early access to sales.
- **Platinum:** reached at AED 30,000 annual spend. Earns a 20% point bonus, free express delivery, and a dedicated support line.

## 3. Redeeming Points
100 points equal AED 5 in redemption value. Points can be redeemed at checkout in store or online, in increments of 100. Points have no cash value and cannot be transferred between accounts.

## 4. Expiry
Points expire 24 months after they are earned if the account has no qualifying activity for 12 consecutive months. Members receive an expiry reminder 30 days before points lapse.

## 5. Returns and Points
When a purchase is refunded, the points earned on it are deducted. If the deduction would take the balance below zero, the balance is set to zero. See the **Customer Returns & Refunds Policy**.

## 6. Account Closure
On account closure, any unredeemed points are forfeited. The Company may suspend or remove points obtained through fraud or system error.
