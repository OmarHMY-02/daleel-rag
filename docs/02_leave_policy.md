# Najmah Retail Group — Leave & Attendance Policy (2026)

This policy details how leave is requested, accrued, and approved. It supplements Section 2 and Section 3 of the **Employee Handbook**.

## 1. Annual Leave
Confirmed full-time employees accrue 30 calendar days of annual leave per year, accruing at 2.5 days per completed month of service. Employees in their probation period (see Handbook Section 3) accrue leave but generally take it only after confirmation. Up to 10 unused leave days may be carried into the next year; days beyond that are forfeited unless the Company requested the deferral.

## 2. Sick Leave
After probation, employees are entitled to up to 90 days of sick leave per year, paid as follows: the first 15 days at full pay, the next 30 days at half pay, and the remaining 45 days unpaid. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than two consecutive days.

## 3. Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 60 days of maternity leave: 45 days at full pay and 15 days at half pay, available after any period of service. An additional period of unpaid leave may be granted for pregnancy-related illness, supported by a medical certificate.

## 4. Parental Leave
All parents are entitled to 5 working days of paid parental leave, to be taken within the first six months following the birth of a child.

## 5. Requesting Leave
Leave is requested through the HR portal at least 7 working days in advance for periods of 3 days or more. Shorter requests require manager discretion. Leave during peak retail periods (Ramadan, Eid, December, and DSF) is subject to roster limits and is approved on a first-come basis.

## 6. Attendance
Repeated unexplained lateness or absence is managed under the disciplinary process referenced in the Handbook. Three instances of unexplained absence in a rolling 12-month period trigger a formal review.

## 7. Public Holidays
Public holidays follow the UAE official calendar. When a public holiday falls on a scheduled rest day, store staff receive a compensatory day within 30 days.
